Our Story

Ajathyan Sri Vari Textiles was established in 2021 with a single purpose — to give families in Koothanallur access to premium-quality sarees and fashion without having to travel to larger cities.

Our family-owned showroom at 190 A.R. Road houses four dedicated shopping floors, each organized by category to make your visit comfortable and productive. From silk bridal sarees to everyday boys' casuals, we've built one store where every family member finds something they love.

We believe that quality clothing shouldn't require a long journey or a large budget. That's why we carefully select every piece in our collection — balancing premium fabric quality with prices that work for families across Koothanallur, Thiruvarur, Mannargudi and surrounding towns.
